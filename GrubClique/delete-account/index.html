Privacy control

Delete your GrubClique account

You can request deletion of your GrubClique account and associated data even if you no longer have the app installed.

Submit a request

Email redxjak@gmail.com with the subject GrubClique account deletion request. Send the request from the email address connected to your account when possible, and include your GrubClique username. We may ask for additional information only when necessary to verify account ownership.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

After verification, we will delete or anonymize your GrubClique authentication account and associated profile, contact-discovery, friendship, clique membership, swipe, match, and chat data. Some limited records may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or resolving an active request. We will explain any applicable retention when responding to you.

Delete inside the Android app

Signed-in users can also open Account settings, select Delete account, and confirm the permanent deletion.

Google sign-in

Deleting your GrubClique account removes the GrubClique account connected to Google. It does not delete your Google account.

Need help?

For questions about deletion or privacy, contact redxjak@gmail.com.
