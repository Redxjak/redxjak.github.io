Privacy

Useful statistics.
Minimal identity.

Effective August 28, 2026. Redxjak websites and browser projects use private, first-party product analytics to understand visits, short-lived sessions, screens, feature usage, broad device category, referring domain, country when available, and sanitized error types.

Each app creates its own random identifier for the current browser session. It is removed when that browser session ends, is not shared with other Redxjak apps, and is not connected to an account. Analytics does not store raw IP addresses, full browser identifiers, URL query values, generated passwords, account identifiers, chat or search content, or other text you enter.

Raw analytics events are stored privately in Supabase and retained indefinitely unless this notice changes. They are visible only through the private owner dashboard and are not sold or used for advertising.

Individual projects may have additional privacy terms for the information required to provide their features. See the GrubClique Privacy Policy for its account and app-specific practices.

Questions can be sent to redxjak@gmail.com.
